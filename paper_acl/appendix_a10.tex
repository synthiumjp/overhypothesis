\section{Recurrence, matched frames, embedding rows and pretrained models (Amendment 10)}\label{app:N}
\setcounter{table}{0}\renewcommand{\thetable}{N\arabic{table}}
\textbf{Registration.} Amendment 10 was registered after the Amendment 9 results and before any of its corpora was used, any of its models was trained, or any pretrained model was run on its items. It answered two confounds raised by a blind review of the Amendment 9 results: familiarity nouns never recurred within a window, and shape-absent nouns never appeared in a frame with a shape slot after \emph{is a}. The noun lists for Part B were fixed, with checksums, before the pilot. A pilot on Pythia-410M (never in a test) gave a mean increase of 0.13 nats from the original to the atypical\_init row, so the smallest effect of interest for H48 was 0.30 nats by the registered rule. One deviation: some Pythia tokenizers define no padding token, so the scoring script falls back to token id 0; padded positions are masked and only the last real token is read, and batched and single-item scores agreed exactly. No score had been produced by the affected models.

\textbf{Part A design.} Everything not stated is as in Amendment 9 (Appendix~\ref{app:M}). Histories: \emph{fixed}, frame 1 (\emph{A \textit{noun} is a \textit{shape} \textit{colour} \textit{texture} thing}) with the noun's own shape; \emph{absent}, frame 7 (\emph{A \textit{noun} is a \textit{colour} \textit{texture} thing}), which names no shape; \emph{rotating}, frame 1 with a shape drawn from the corpus's shape distribution, a new one for every sentence when presented singly and one per run, changing between runs, when presented in runs. Presentation: \emph{single}, one sentence per unit; \emph{run}, four consecutive sentences about the noun in one window. Exposures (100 or 1,000) count sentences, so runs and singles are matched in sentences, not in units or spacing. Trained kinds use frame 7 in one sentence in fifteen, so the frame is not new. Three nouns per cell (12 cells) and four unseen nouns; Regular grouped (confirmatory) and Reassigned grouped (exploratory) base corpora; three sizes and ten seeds (60 models). A checker re-read every corpus and verified exposures, units, frames, shapes within runs and window placement; all 40 corpora matched checksums recorded before training.

\textbf{Part C (embedding rows in the Amendment 9 models).} In the 60 final grouped models of Amendment 9 we replaced the input rows of unseen nouns and scored carry again; the unmodified condition reproduced the Amendment 9 scores. Conditions: own row; own direction scaled to the mean norm of the trained kinds' rows (ownnorm); a random direction at that norm (randnorm); the trained kinds' mean row. Shape-absent nouns at 10,000 exposures were also scored with their own direction scaled to the unseen nouns' mean norm (shrunk).

\textbf{Part B (pretrained models).} 44 nouns in 22 pairs, matched on Pile frequency and count/mass type, were coded for colour typicality by two independent AI passes with a fixed prompt before any model was run (exact agreement 94\%, weighted $\kappa$ = 0.96); nouns that are also colour terms or that both passes flagged as polysemous were excluded. Colour-typical nouns are mostly natural kinds, so kind was a covariate. Each noun was read in three neutral frames with each of the eleven basic colour terms that are one token; carry is the mean over frames and ordered colour pairs of $\log p(c_1 \mid c_1) - \log p(c_1 \mid c_2)$, with colour probabilities renormalised over the colour set. Only the noun's input row was changed.
