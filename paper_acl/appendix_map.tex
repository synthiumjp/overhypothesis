\textbf{Hypotheses.} Table~\ref{tab:hyp} lists every registered hypothesis of Experiments 1 and 2, the row analysis and the pretrained test, the claim of the main text it bears on, and its outcome under the registered decision rules (Holm correction within each experiment; direction in at least two of three model sizes; for the pretrained test, Holm within each model and support in at least four of five models). Equivalence bounds were set in each registration: $\pm$1 nat in Amendment 9 (about one seventh of the 7.6-nat fall from unseen to typical embeddings in the earlier experiments); in Amendment 10, $\pm$0.3 nats for H40, H41 and H44 (the smallest history effect seen in Amendment 9), $\pm$1 nat for the check K1, and the larger of 0.3 nats and one fifth of the pilot effect for H48. The hypotheses of the earlier experiments are in Appendix~\ref{app:A}.
